Independent Contractor Model

Freedom to teach.
Structure to grow.

All coach relationships at Arenna Denver are structured as 1099 independent contractor agreements. You own your schedule, your client relationships, and your growth. We provide the platform, the methodology, and the operation.

  • Set your own session schedule within platform availability
  • Build and retain your own student base on court
  • Develop your personal coaching brand within the Arenna framework
  • All earnings reported on 1099 — no employment restrictions or overhead
  • Full access to Arenna Denver's facilities, courts, and systems
  • Training and certification in the Arenna Denver padel methodology
  • Priority consideration for leadership roles as we expand to new locations
